Privacy policy

At Algory, our mission is to make it radically easier for anyone to create content worth watching. We build tools that take a creator from a blank page to a finished video — writing the script, holding it steady on camera, and learning what works for that specific person over time — so that consistency and ideas, rather than budget or crew, decide who gets heard.

We at Algory ("Algory", "we", "our" or "us") are committed to respecting your privacy and to keeping secure any information we obtain from you or about you. This Privacy Policy describes our practices with respect to personal data that we collect from or about you, and how we use it when you use our mobile application and related services (collectively, "Services").

Algory is operated by Sucevan Alexandrina, based in Romania, who is the controller responsible for the processing of your personal data as described in this Privacy Policy.

If you use the Services without creating an account, we may store some of the information described in this Policy on your device, for example to remember the preferences you set during onboarding.

1. Personal data we collect

We collect personal data relating to you ("Personal Data") as follows:

Personal Data you provide

  • Account Information: When you create an account with us, we collect your email address and account credentials. If you sign in using Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In, we receive the name and email address associated with that account, as permitted by your settings with that provider. We never receive or store your password for those accounts.
  • Creator Profile: When you set up Algory, we collect the answers you give us about your work as a creator — your content niche, the platforms you publish on, how much time you spend editing, the goals you are working toward, and the difficulty you are currently facing. We use this to tailor what Algory suggests to you.
  • User Content: We collect the Personal Data you provide in the input to our Services ("Content"), including the messages you send to the Algory assistant, the scripts and notes you write or edit in the app, the video recordings you choose to send into a conversation, and anything else you choose to share with us.
  • Communication Information: If you contact us by email, we collect your name, contact details, and the contents of your message.

Personal Data we receive from your use of the Services

  • Log Data: Information our servers record automatically when the app makes a request to them, such as the date and time of the request, which we need to keep the Services running and secure.
  • Usage Data: Information about your activity in the app, such as the features you use and the actions you take — for example the scripts you ask for and the videos you have captioned or cut — which helps us understand what is working and what is not.
  • Purchase Information: If you subscribe to a plan, we receive your subscription status and purchase history (which plan you have, when it started, and when it renews or ends), so that we can give you the features of your plan. Payments are handled by Apple; we never receive your payment card details.

Video recordings

Our Teleprompter records video using your device's camera and microphone. After recording, you can review the result in the app and decide whether to keep it or discard it and record again. A recording you discard is deleted from your device and is never sent to us.

A recording leaves your device only when you choose to send it into a conversation — for example, to have Algory add captions, cut pauses, or otherwise edit it for you.

When you do, the recording is uploaded to our infrastructure so that it can be processed on our servers and the result returned to you. The video itself is not sent to our artificial intelligence provider (OpenAI).

When you ask Algory to add captions to a video or to cut its pauses, the audio of that recording is sent to AssemblyAI, a speech-to-text provider, which returns a transcript of what you said together with the time of each word. We use the transcript to write your captions and to make sure a cut never removes or splits a word you said. We keep that transcript with your video, so that you can try another caption style without transcribing the video again, and as soon as we have received it we ask AssemblyAI to delete the audio and the transcript from its servers. AssemblyAI does not use audio data from this app to train its models — we have opted out of their model improvement program.

We keep the recordings you send, and the videos we make from them (cut or captioned), for 30 days after they were last used, so that you have time to watch and download the result. After that they are deleted automatically from our servers. Deleting your account removes them earlier, as described in Section 7.

2. How we use Personal Data

We may use Personal Data for the following purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and improve our Services;
  • To understand how the Services are used and improve them, for example by looking at which features are used and, when needed to fix a problem or improve quality, at the messages and recordings you send;
  • To transcribe the recordings you send, to add captions to your videos, and to cut their pauses;
  • To manage your subscription and give you the features of your plan;
  • To personalize the scripts, hooks, and guidance Algory produces for you, and to remember the goals and context you have shared so that Algory can support you consistently over time;
  • To authenticate your account and keep it secure;
  • To communicate with you, including about changes to our Services or this Policy;
  • To prevent fraud, abuse, and activity that violates our terms;
  • To comply with legal obligations and to protect the rights, privacy, safety, or property of our users, Algory, or third parties.

We do not sell your Personal Data, and we do not use it to serve you third-party advertising.

3. Legal bases for processing

Where the General Data Protection Regulation or similar law applies, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract: to provide the Services you signed up for, including adding captions to a video or cutting its pauses when you ask us to, and providing the plan you subscribed to.
  • Consent: where you have given it, such as when you share your creator profile or enable optional features, or when you agree to this Policy and ask us to transcribe the voice in a video you send.
  • Legitimate interests: to keep the Services secure, functional, and improving, where those interests are not overridden by your rights.
  • Legal obligation: where we are required to process data to comply with the law.

We transcribe your voice only to produce your captions and to cut the pauses of your videos. We do not use it to identify you.

4. How we share Personal Data

We do not share your Personal Data except in the circumstances below.

  • Service providers: We share data with vendors who process it on our behalf in order to operate the Services. Your conversations with the Algory assistant are sent to a third-party artificial intelligence provider (OpenAI) to generate responses; the audio of the recordings you ask us to caption or cut is sent to a speech-to-text provider (AssemblyAI) to be transcribed; our account data, creator profiles, recordings, transcripts and edited videos are hosted by our infrastructure provider (Supabase); the processing that adds captions to your videos and cuts them runs on cloud computing infrastructure operated by a hosting provider on our behalf; and your subscription status and purchase history, together with your account identifier, are processed by our subscription management provider (RevenueCat). These providers act on our instructions and are bound by contractual obligations covering confidentiality and security.
  • Authentication providers: If you sign in with Apple or Google, those companies process your authentication data under their own privacy policies.
  • Payments: Subscriptions are bought and billed through Apple's App Store. Apple processes your payment under its own privacy policy; we never receive your payment card details.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose Personal Data if required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request, or where we believe disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of our users, Algory, or the public.
  • Business transfers: If Algory is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, Personal Data may be transferred as part of that transaction, and we will notify you of any change in control of your information.

5. AI-generated output

Algory uses artificial intelligence, including third-party language models, to produce scripts, hooks, feedback, and suggestions in response to what you write. Output is generated, not retrieved from a verified source, and may be inaccurate, incomplete, or unsuitable for your situation. You are responsible for reviewing it and applying your own judgment before publishing or acting on it.

Algory is not a substitute for professional advice. Nothing it produces should be relied on as financial, medical, legal, or mental health guidance.

6. International transfers

We operate from Romania. The providers described in Section 4, including AssemblyAI, RevenueCat and the provider that hosts our video processing, may process data on servers located outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Where that happens, we rely on contractual safeguards with those providers intended to give your Personal Data a level of protection consistent with applicable data protection law.

7. Retention

We retain Personal Data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with the Services. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete or anonymize the associated Personal Data within a reasonable period, except where we are required to retain it by law, or where retention is necessary to resolve disputes or enforce our agreements.

Recordings you send and the edited videos we make from them are deleted automatically 30 days after they were last used (sent, cut or captioned), or earlier when you delete your account. Transcripts are kept in your account until you delete your account, when they are removed with it. We ask AssemblyAI to delete the audio we send and its transcript as soon as we have received the transcript; in any case, AssemblyAI deletes the audio within 48 hours and the transcript within 30 days.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to our processing of your Personal Data, to receive a copy of it in a portable format, and to withdraw consent you have previously given. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

To exercise these rights, email us at support@getalgory.app. We will respond within the period required by applicable law.

If you are in the European Union, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority. In Romania, this is the National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing (ANSPDCP).

9. Children

Our Services are not directed to, and we do not knowingly collect Personal Data from, children under 13. If we learn that we have collected Personal Data from a child under 13, we will delete it. Users aged 13 to 17 may use the Services, and we encourage parents and guardians to be aware of and involved in how young people use AI-powered applications. If you believe a child has provided us with Personal Data without the consent of a parent or guardian, contact us at support@getalgory.app.

10. Security

We implement commercially reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect Personal Data against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration, including the use of established infrastructure providers and controls on who can access your data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. When we do, we will post the updated version with a new date at the top of this page and, where the changes are material, notify you through the app or by email before they take effect.

12. Contact

Questions or requests about this Policy or your Personal Data can be sent to support@getalgory.app.
