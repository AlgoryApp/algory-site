Quick answers

How do subscriptions work?

Algory's plans are sold and billed through the Apple App Store. A subscription renews automatically at the end of each period unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before the period ends.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → Algory → Cancel Subscription. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do captions work?

Send a video in the chat and ask Algory to add captions, in your own words. With your permission, the voice in the video is turned into text by a speech-to-text service; Algory then adds the captions in the style you chose and sends the finished video back in the chat a few minutes later, ready to save to Photos.
